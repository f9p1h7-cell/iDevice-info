Restore/Downgrade a 32-bit device to iOS 8.4.1/6.1.3

Downgrade

OTA downgrade path for supported 32-bit hardware (such as iPhone 4S, iPhone 5, iPad 2, and iPod touch 5th gen depending on target version).

You are supposed to use Legacy iOS Kit with this!!
  • The procedure will be depending on your usage, operating system, and iOS device. Make sure that your setup is supported by the script. See the README for details.
  • You do NOT need blobs for iOS 8.4.1/6.1.3, the script will get them for you.
  • The restore will ERASE ALL DATA on your iOS device. Backup your data before proceeding.
  • Make sure to have free space on your PC/Mac for the IPSW files.
  • Do not rename the IPSW firmwares from their default names.
  • If your device is not on the latest iOS version, I recommend saving onboard blobs first before continuing.
  • "A6(X)" refers to iPhone 5 and iPad 4.
  • "A5(X)" refers to iPhone 4S, iPad 2, iPad 3, iPad mini 1, and iPod touch 5th gen.
  • If the option has "jailbroken", jailbreak your device first. If not, jailbreaking is not required.
  • If the script always fails to detect your device, try rebooting your PC/Mac.

For devices jailbroken with Pangu

  • For devices using Pangu and jailbroken method, you need to install the latest untether for your iOS version first before continuing.

Please select your iOS device.

All devices, jailbroken on iOS 9 or lower

  • Note: If you used EverPwnage to jailbreak, you may start from the 4th step since OpenSSH is pre-installed (unless you disabled it)
  1. Open Cydia on your device, and wait for sources to refresh.
    • If refreshing takes too long, remove the ultrasn0w and ZodTTD repos, force close Cydia, then open Cydia again.
  2. When it asks to upgrade, you can choose to not upgrade anything (Ignore), it is optional.
  3. Search for "OpenSSH" and install it.
  4. Plug in your device and start the script by running restore.sh
  5. When the main menu shows up, select Restore/Downgrade.
  6. Select your preferred option/iOS version.
  7. Download/select the IPSW(s) needed to continue.
  8. Select Start Restore.
  9. When prompted, select your options and follow on-screen instructions to put the device to kDFU/pwnDFU mode.
  10. After the restore process, your device should be successfully downgraded/restored to your selected target version.

A6(X) devices, jailbroken on iOS 10

  • You need to jailbreak with socket to follow these steps.
  1. Open Zebra on your device, and wait for sources to refresh.
  2. Add my repository: https://lukezgd.github.io/repo/
    • Go to Sources, then tap '+' at the top left.
  3. Search and install "Dropbear" from my repo.
  4. Plug in your device and start the script by running restore.sh
  5. When the main menu shows up, select Restore/Downgrade.
  6. Select your preferred option/iOS version.
  7. Download/select the IPSW(s) needed to continue.
  8. Select Start Restore.
  9. When prompted, select your options and follow on-screen instructions to put the device to kDFU/pwnDFU mode.
  10. After the restore process, your device should be successfully downgraded/restored to your selected target version.

A5(X) devices, not jailbroken

  • It is highly recommended to jailbreak your device and follow the "jailbroken" steps instead of doing this. Only use this if you are unable to jailbreak at all for some reason.
  • You need to have an Arduino and USB Host Shield or a Raspberry Pi Pico in order to use checkm8-a5. Go to the "checkm8-a5" page for more details.

Older devices and A6(X) devices, not jailbroken

  • A6(X) devices: It is highly recommended to jailbreak your device and follow the "jailbroken" steps instead of doing this. Only use this if you are unable to jailbreak at all for some reason.
  • You NEED a working Home and Top/Power button to enter DFU mode to follow this.
  • If your button/s are broken and you are unable to jailbreak nor enter DFU mode, see here for details about "DFU IPSW"
  1. Plug in your device and place your device in DFU mode.
  2. Start the script by running restore.sh
  3. When the main menu shows up, select Restore/Downgrade.
  4. When prompted, select your options and follow the given instructions to put the device to pwnDFU mode.
    • If you are using Linux, it will likely fail a lot here for A6(X) devices. It is recommended to follow the jailbroken steps instead.
    • If you have a spare iOS device that is jailbroken, you can use iPwnder Lite for iOS
  5. After the restore process, your device will be successfully downgraded/restored to your selected target version.
  • If you are having trouble placing the device in pwnDFU mode, proceed here for alternatives.

Restore/Downgrade (and hacktivate) an iPhone 2G/3G/3GS/4/4S, iPod touch 1/2/3/4, iPad 1

Legacy 32-bit

Covers restoring, downgrading, and hacktivating early 30-pin and early Lightning 32-bit iPhones, iPod touches, and the 1st-gen iPad without existing blobs.

You are supposed to use Legacy iOS Kit with this!!
  • The procedure will be depending on your usage, operating system, and iOS device. Make sure that your setup is supported by the script. See the README for details.
  • You do NOT need existing blobs to use this.
  • This page for the restoring (and jailbreaking) the following devices without existing blobs:
    • iPhone 3G/3GS/4/4S, iPad 1, iPod touch 2/3/4 - latest/lower iOS versions
    • iPhone 2G, iPod touch 1st gen - iOS 3.1.3
    • For restoring with SHSH blobs, go to: Restore-32-bit-Device
  • iPhone 2G, 3G, and 3GS also have the option to hacktivate for the custom IPSW
  • The restore will ERASE ALL DATA on your iOS device. Backup your data before proceeding.
  • Make sure to have at least 10 GB of free space on your PC/Mac.
  • Do not rename the IPSW firmwares from their default names.
  • If you are on an iOS version that is not the latest, I recommend saving onboard blobs first before continuing.
  • You NEED a working Home and Top/Power button to enter DFU mode when needed.
    • If your button/s are broken, you can use a DFU IPSW to force the device to DFU mode. This will also clear all device data, so backup data and SHSH blobs before proceeding.
    • To proceed using a DFU IPSW, run the script and go to: Restore/Downgrade -> DFU IPSW

Please select your operating system and iOS device.

iPhone 4 GSM and CDMA, iPad 1, iPod touch 3 - restore (and jailbreak) with powdersn0w

  • For iPhone 4, it must be a GSM or CDMA model (iPhone3,1 or 3,3) to use powdersn0w on Legacy iOS Kit. iPhone3,2 is not supported.
  1. Connect your iOS device to your PC/Mac.
  2. Start the script by running restore.sh
  3. When the main menu shows up, select Restore/Downgrade -> powdersn0w.
  4. Select Base IPSW.
    • For the iPhone 4, download and select iOS 7.1.2 IPSW.
    • For the iPad 1 and touch 3, download and select iOS 5.1.1 IPSW.
  5. Select Target IPSW. This will be the IPSW for the iOS version that you want to restore/downgrade to.
  6. When prompted, select your options and follow the given instructions to put the device to kDFU/pwnDFU mode.
  7. After the restore process, your device will be successfully downgraded/restored to your selected target version.
  • If you are stuck at the boot logo after downgrading, see the "Clearing NVRAM" section of the "Troubleshooting" wiki page for more details.

All other devices - restore (and jailbreak) to latest/lower iOS version

  • Note: The iOS versions you can restore to are limited depending on your device.
  1. Plug in your device and place your device in DFU mode.
  2. Start the script by running restore.sh
  3. When the main menu shows up, select Restore/Downgrade -> Your preferred iOS version.
  4. When prompted, select your options and follow the given instructions to put the device to pwnDFU mode.
  5. After the restore process, your device will be successfully restored to your target iOS version.

A12 A13 Tethered

Tethered / Research

Notes and workflows for A12 and A13 devices where tethered or exploit-specific conditions apply.

You are supposed to use Legacy iOS Kit with this!!

Legacy iOS Kit supports tethered downgrades and restores on A12/A13 devices compatible with usbliter8. The a12a13 module is heavily based on surrealra1n. Credits to pwnerblu and all surrealra1n contributors for their work.

Supported Devices

  • iPhone XS, XR, 11, and SE 2020, including Pro and Max variants
  • iPad mini 5
  • iPad Air 3

Supported Versions

  • A12: iOS 14.0 beta 4 through iOS 16.6.1
  • A13: iOS 13.4 through iOS 16.6.1
  • Note: Support for iOS 16.1 and newer has been added thanks to libdmg-apfs by NSSpiral (LukeZGD fork for fixes and build)

Additional Steps for iOS 14 and 15 Restores

When restoring to iOS 14 or 15, complete the following procedure before restoring to your desired version:

  1. Restore to iOS 14 beta 4 (build 18A5342e). For A13 devices, an iOS 13.x version can also be used.
  2. Perform a tethered boot using Just Boot.
  3. Complete device activation during setup.
  4. Once activation is complete, restore to your desired iOS 14 or 15 version.

This procedure is not required when restoring to iOS 13 or 16.

Tethered Restore/Downgrade

This is a tethered restore/downgrade. You will need a PC/Mac and a usbliter8-compatible board to pwn the device and boot iOS after every reboot or power-off.

To boot the device, select Just Boot from the main menu, or run the following command, replacing <ios build id> with the target iOS build ID:

./restore.sh --just-boot --build-id=<ios build id>

For example, to boot iOS 16.0.3 (build 20A392):

./restore.sh --just-boot --build-id=20A392

TrollStore

TrollStore is the recommended way to install the jailbreak apps on iOS 14-16. The installation method depends on the target iOS version:

  • iOS 14 and 15: Install TrollStore using the TrollHelperOTA method.
  • iOS 16: Use the Install TrollStore option in the Legacy iOS Kit main menu, which uses the TrollRestore method.

iOS 13 does not support TrollStore. Instead, sideload the jailbreak app. See the Sideload Method in the Jailbreaking wiki page.

Jailbreaking

Use the following tools to jailbreak your device, depending on the iOS version:

  • iOS 15 and 16: pwnerblu's Dopamine fork. Regular Dopamine builds are known to crash on these tethered restores, so use this fork instead.
  • iOS 14: Taurine
  • iOS 13: Amethyst

Broken Device Features and Info

The following limitations are based on information from surrealra1n and may vary depending on the iOS version and device.

iOS 13

  • SEP: Partially incompatible.
  • Passcode and Touch/Face ID: Cannot be configured because of BPR enforcement.
  • Home button: The Haptic Home button will not work, and the AssistiveTouch Home button option will not appear.
  • iPhone 11 models: Should be largely functional, with the exceptions of Ultra Wideband (UWB), Face ID, and passcode functionality.

iOS 14 and 15

  • Activation: SEP is partially incompatible, so the device cannot be activated after restoring without first completing the preliminary procedure described above.
  • Passcode and Touch/Face ID: Cannot be configured because of BPR enforcement.
  • Sideloading: Installing apps outside of TrollStore may not work.
  • Other issues: Additional features may be broken due to SEP incompatibility.

iOS 16

  • Passcode and Touch/Face ID: Cannot be configured because of BPR enforcement.
  • Haptic Home button: Will not work.
  • Activation: The device should activate normally. Restoring to iOS 13.x or iOS 14 beta 4 is not necessary.
  • Developer Mode: Enabled automatically during the restore; manual activation is not required.

Pwning Using Another iOS Device

Exploit

Exploiting or entering DFU/pwned state on a target iDevice using a second iOS device instead of a desktop computer.

You are supposed to use Legacy iOS Kit with this!!
  • You can use another iOS device that is jailbroken to put your target device in pwned DFU mode.
  • This can be very useful if you do not have a Mac but have a spare iOS device on hand.
  • This method can be used for 3GS, touch 3, A4, A6(X), and 64-bit (A7-A11) target devices.
  • In the picture, the iPhone 7 on the right is the host device where iPwnder Lite is installed and USB lightning to camera adapter is plugged in. It is jailbroken with Dopamine.
  • The iPhone 5S on the left is the target device I will be pwning with checkm8, plugged into the iPhone 7 via the USB adapter with a lightning to USB-A cable. I have also placed it in DFU mode.
  1. On your host device, install iPwnder Lite, and follow the setup in the picture above
    • Get iPwnder Lite from my repo: https://lukezgd.github.io/repo
    • For pwning 64-bit (A7-A11) target devices, get gaster instead
  2. Install and open NewTerm or MTerminal on the host device
  3. Run this command: ipwnder
    • For pwning 64-bit (A7-A11) target devices, run this command: gaster pwn
  4. If successful, this will put the target device in pwned DFU mode
  5. Once the target device is now in pwned DFU mode, plug it back to the PC/Mac and proceed with Legacy iOS Kit usage

Notes

  • iPwnder Lite and gaster should work on all jailbreaks for iOS 9 and newer.
  • For iOS 15 and newer, the tools have been tested successfully on palera1n and Dopamine.
  • If your host device has a USB-C port (eg. newer iPads), use a USB-A to USB-C adapter instead of a USB-A to lightning adapter.
  • Do not use lightning to USB-C cables, they will not work. Only use lightning to USB A cables.
